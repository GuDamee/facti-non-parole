Facti, non parole.

In Italy, entry-level hiring dropped by 18.8% in one year. Also, an international survey shows that 88% of new workers feel unprepared.

The problem is not just finding a job. It is showing how you work. A CV does not show how you communicate, collaborate, or solve unexpected problems.

That is why we created Facti, non parole. It is a 3–6 month program by Factanza Academy to help you move from university to your first job.

  • 🔹 24 hours online on soft skills and personal branding.
  • 🔹 At least 36 hours in a company on real problems. Final project, tutor feedback, and a portfolio.

To start: an interview with an AI avatar (€5). No grades: it identifies your hard skill category. Then you have a human interview. Those admitted pay €200.

We do not promise a job. We promise that a company will see you work.

Facti, non parole. For hiring managers too.

Companies demand experience for 93.4% of graduate jobs. But how can a fresh graduate get experience?

With Facti, non parole, the new Factanza Academy path, young people work on a real problem of your company before any possible hiring.

  • 🔹 They arrive trained: 24 online hours on soft skills and personal branding with Factanza.
  • 🔹 An AI avatar interview identifies hard skill categories with no grades. Human evaluators step in next.
  • 🔹 At least 36 practice hours with you, online or in person. You can add more hours.
  • 🔹 A final task and your feedback on observed behaviors. See how a candidate thinks, communicates, and works in a team before hiring.

We are looking for our first partner companies, including SMEs. Contact us.
