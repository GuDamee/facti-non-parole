The final test in person

PROPOSAL

  • Groups of 3-4 students work on a real company problem.
  • Half a day or a full day, at the company.
  • Company tutors observe with a grid: communication, teamwork, objections, timing.
  • 5-minute group pitch, then the tutor asks each person questions.
  • Written feedback from the company in the portfolio.
  • The company confirms, not Factanza.

And this quiz?

It is the theory gate: it closes the online course and comes before the practice.

If you pass it, you are ready for practice in the company.

It is not the final evaluation. The company does that, in person.

The course and the exam

Online course · 24 h

Modules · PROPOSAL

  1. Written and spoken communication
  2. Giving and receiving feedback
  3. Teamwork and conflicts
  4. Handling objections
  5. Time and priorities
  6. Presenting: the pitch
  7. Personal brand and LinkedIn
  8. Portfolio
  9. The first weeks in a company

How the exam works

  • 15 questions on the course modules.
  • Each question has only one right answer.
  • After each answer you see why it is right or not.
  • Pass mark: 70%, that is 11 out of 15 (PROPOSAL).
  • If you don't pass, you review and you can retake it.
  • It is not the final evaluation: that is the in-person test at the company.
  • No promise of hiring.

Time: about 5-6 minutes.
